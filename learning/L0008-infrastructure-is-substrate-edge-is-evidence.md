# L0008 Infrastructure is substrate, edge is evidence

## Why This Mattered

RoboWeather can complete sophisticated collectors, replay, modeling, and execution machinery while still having no forward-profitable strategy; project work needs direct attribution to the economic term it is intended to improve.

## What Happened

After the tape and deterministic discovery substrate became operational, broad causal discovery still produced zero untouched-holdout survivors and model probabilities remained overconfident. The project therefore adopted four explicit net-edge pillars instead of treating system completion as progress toward profitability.

## Concept

Decompose net trading edge into information advantage, settlement advantage, execution advantage, and costs/adverse selection, and require every bounded task to name the pillar whose evidence it changes.

## Intuition

A faster engine is valuable only if the car is pointed somewhere worth going. Infrastructure creates the ability to test an edge; it is not itself proof that an edge exists.

## General Pattern

This applies to research platforms, recommender systems, fraud systems, and trading stacks whenever observability or technical sophistication can be mistaken for outcome improvement.

## RoboWeather Application

Tag every new RoboWeather thread and hypothesis with a primary pillar, close it with a pillar-scoped conclusion, and require all four pillars to clear together before funded promotion.

## Questions To Revisit

Are pillar tags actually preventing vague infrastructure work? Which pillar is now the limiting constraint? Are cross-pillar threads rare and genuinely integrative?
